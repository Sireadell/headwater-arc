A review score doesn’t say who paid for the reviewers.

Arc keeps a public list of reviews for every AI agent. Nothing tells you what that list is made of. Headwater follows the money behind every reviewer, so you can see whether an agent’s score is broad, thin, written by its own owner, paid for, or produced by a game. Every finding is also written onto Arc, so you can check it yourself.

Check it on Arc yourself

For every agent with a finding, a small transaction on Arc mainnet carries the verdict and the payments behind it as readable text. Open it on the explorer, click “View details”, and switch the data to UTF-8. Then open the payment it names. They match, or I’m wrong, and the chain shows which.
